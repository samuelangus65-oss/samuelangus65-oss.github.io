About Samuel Angus

A life of working with people.
Formed across every sector that matters.

Samuel Angus brings to coaching a breadth of working experience that spans decades and crosses almost every sector that touches human need, human potential, and human systems. Retail at senior consultant level. Local authority finance, housing, and cashiering. Third sector project management — including leading a local employment project supporting people with mental health difficulties back into work, in partnership with Jobcentre Plus. Youth justice work through the probation service, supporting young people under RAP with life skills and direction. Supplementary teaching in Maths and English, and founding and running his own supplementary school preparing children for their SATs in English, Maths, and Science. Serving on management committees across third sector organisations. Working with inequality and discrimination reduction teams across statutory services — including police forces and housing organisations. And over two decades within the NHS — across PALS, carer development, the carers team, systemic family therapy, diversity and equality, and as a Cultural Inclusion Ambassador working across the Trust on disciplinary and grievance proceedings.

"It is not one system that has formed me. It is a life spent inside the real complexity of how people, communities, and organisations work — and what they need to thrive."

Now, as an ILM Level 5 Certified Coach, Samuel brings that full formation to personal and professional development coaching. Drawing on systemic thinking, reflexive questioning, and a genuine commitment to seeing the person in front of him clearly — he helps individuals and organisations find the direction, clarity, and understanding they already carry within them.

The Coaching Approach

The counsel you need
is already within you.
It simply needs drawing out.

Every coaching engagement begins with the belief that the person in front of me carries more than they have yet been able to access. The work is not to advise, instruct, or prescribe. It is to create the conditions in which what is already within you can surface — through honest reflection, careful questioning, and genuine human presence.

01

We start with your whole picture

Before goals, we explore context. Who and what shapes your situation? What patterns keep returning? What remains unspoken in your team or organisation? Understanding the whole system is where genuine clarity begins.

02

We work with what is real

Coaching is not about ideal scenarios. It is about your actual situation, with all its complexity. Through reflexive and circular questioning, we surface what is genuinely happening — and what you most want to change.

03

We move toward lasting direction

Reflection without movement is just thinking. Together we move from understanding to decision, from hesitation to purposeful action. The clarity you develop in our sessions is designed to hold well beyond them.

The Coaching Toolkit

The right approach for each person — not a fixed formula

No two clients are the same. I draw on a range of evidence-based frameworks and approaches — selecting what is most appropriate for each individual, team, or organisational context. The structure of our coaching adapts to your needs. What remains constant is the quality of thinking, the depth of the conversation, and the commitment to real, lasting change.
