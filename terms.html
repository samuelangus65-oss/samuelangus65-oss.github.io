About me

1What coaching is, and what it is not

Coaching is a structured, forward-looking conversation that helps you gain clarity, set goals, and move toward what you want. I work on the principle that the answers are usually already within you, and my role is to help draw them out.

Coaching is not therapy, counselling, or medical care. It is not a treatment for mental health conditions, and it is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

If something comes up in our work that would be better supported by a different kind of professional, I will say so. Where appropriate, I may suggest you speak with your GP, a counsellor, or another suitable professional. You remain responsible for your own decisions, wellbeing, and actions.

2Sessions and how we work

Coaching sessions are delivered online via Zoom. Your first conversation is a 45-minute Discovery Session. Ongoing coaching is delivered through the Momentum Coaching Programme: a fortnightly live session via Zoom (50 minutes), alternating with a guided reflection week in which you receive a personalised prompt and a voice note response from me. Messaging support is available throughout as described below.

You are responsible for joining sessions at the agreed time, in a private space with a reliable connection. If you are running late, the session will still end at the scheduled time so as not to affect others.

3Messaging support between sessions

Weekly coaching includes messaging support between sessions, so you are never carrying things alone. To keep this sustainable and effective, please note:

  • Messaging is for brief support, reflection, and accountability between sessions, not for live or urgent conversations.
  • I aim to respond within normal working hours, Monday to Sunday.
  • I take one rest day each week, which can change on a monthly basis. Messages received on that day are replied to the following day.
  • Messaging is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis or need urgent help, please contact your GP, NHS 111, the emergency services, or a crisis line such as the Samaritans on 116 123.

4Fees and payment

My current fees are shown on my website. The Discovery Session is charged at the rate shown at the time of booking. Weekly coaching is charged at the standard or concession rate as agreed.

Payment is due in advance of each session or as otherwise agreed. Sessions are confirmed once payment is received.

5Concession rate eligibility

I offer a concession rate to make coaching more accessible to three groups: students, NHS staff (including those recently retired), and those on a low income. The concession rate is offered in good faith and on the following basis:

  • Eligibility must be confirmed. For the student and NHS staff rates, I may ask for reasonable evidence, for example a valid student ID, an NHS email address, or proof of employment. For the low income rate, which is intended for people on a low income or unwaged, including for example those receiving Universal Credit or similar support and those in low-paid or insecure work, no documentation is required; eligibility is agreed together in good faith through a brief, confidential conversation at the discovery stage.
  • Verification at the first session. The concession rate is provisional until eligibility is confirmed. If confirmation is not provided, the standard rate will apply from the next session.
  • Annual re-confirmation. Continued eligibility for the concession rate is reviewed each year. I may ask you to confirm you still meet the criteria.
  • Providing false information to obtain a concession rate is a breach of these Terms and may end our working relationship.
  • Concession places are limited. To maintain the sustainability of the practice and ensure every client receives the same quality of care, concession places are limited to a small proportion of my overall caseload at any one time. If concession places are currently full when you enquire, I will let you know and invite you to join a waiting list. This is a practical and transparent measure, not a barrier to access.

6Cancellation and rescheduling

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give at least 24 hours' notice through the booking link in your confirmation, so the time can be offered to someone else.

  • Sessions cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice can be moved to another time.
  • Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without notice, may be charged in full.
  • If I ever need to cancel or reschedule a session, I will give you as much notice as possible and offer an alternative time at no extra cost.

Wherever possible, I would discuss this with you first.
