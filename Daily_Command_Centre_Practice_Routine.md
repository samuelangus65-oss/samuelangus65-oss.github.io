# Daily Command Centre Practice Routine
### Samuel Angus Coaching

**Purpose:** a few minutes each morning to build real confidence with the
Command Centre's judgement calls, the same disciplined daily habit that
worked for learning the OTA platforms, applied here to your own tool
rather than someone else's algorithm.

---

## 3/3/3, in simple terms

Each day, sort what you need to do into three small buckets, no more than
three things in each:

- **3 Deep Work things** (Navy) — tasks needing real focus and a clear
  head. Not chores, the stuff that actually moves something forward.
- **3 Shorter Tasks** (Teal) — quick things done without much thinking, a
  phone call, an eBay listing, a quick email.
- **3 Admin things** (Gold) — low-energy paperwork and tidying that
  doesn't need your best brain, just needs doing.

Nine things maximum, in three clear groups. The point is never facing one
long list of fifteen things and freezing, always three small, manageable
groups instead.

---

## Daily Command Centre practice routine

**1. Open Today's Loop first — 30 seconds**
Just glance. Anyone flagged? Nothing to overthink, just notice.

**2. Pick one client folder to practise on — 5 minutes**
A real coachee if you have one that day, or a practice scenario (like
Alex) if not. Before touching any step, ask yourself: *what does this
person actually need right now?* Then check your own instinct against
Steps 0 through 3, in that order:

- **Step 0 (Five Whys)** — only when the presenting problem feels
  surface-level and something underneath hasn't been named yet. Skip if
  you already understand what's going on.
- **Step 0.5 (Framework Decision Map)** — one deliberate choice once you
  have a real sense of what's needed, not a menu to browse.
- **Step 1 (Tomm's positions)** — a quick check: Customer, Complainant,
  or Visitor. Changes how directive you can safely be.
- **Step 2 (Stress signals)** — a genuine safety check, not routine. Slow
  down properly here whenever something feels off.
- **Step 3 (Struggle pattern)** — worth real thought, since this
  calibrates the message's tone and different patterns need different
  responses even when they look similar on the surface.

**3. One line in your own notes: what felt unclear? — 1 minute**
If anything about the tool felt clunky, confusing, or slow, write it
down. This is the snagging list building itself from real use, not
guesswork.

**4. Stop.**
Do not run every step on every client every day. That is not practice,
that is noise. Let your own judgement decide which steps are actually
needed, the tool is scaffolding for that judgement, not a replacement
for it.

---

## The one rule underneath all of this

Struggling Coachee Support is for when something is stuck, not for
routine contact. The regular generators (Midweek Check-in, Pre-Session
Reminder, and so on) are for the normal rhythm of the programme. Reach
for the deeper tool only when your gut says something needs more care
than a standard touchpoint.
