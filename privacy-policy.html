Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This privacy policy explains how Samuel Angus Personal & Professional Development Coach collects, uses, and protects your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Please read it carefully before using this website or engaging coaching services.

Section 01

Who we are

Samuel Angus

As a sole trader coaching practice, Samuel Angus is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller.

Section 02

What personal data we collect

We collect only the personal data necessary to provide coaching services and maintain professional records. This may include:

  • →Your full name and contact details — email address, phone number
  • →Booking information — session dates, times, and meeting details collected via Calendly
  • →Session notes — written notes taken during or after coaching sessions
  • →Information you share voluntarily during coaching — personal, professional, or organisational context
  • →Payment information — processed securely through bank transfer or approved payment providers
  • →Website usage data — standard analytics data collected when you visit this website
Section 03

How we use your personal data

Personal data, including name, contact details, and session notes, is collected and processed for the purpose of delivering coaching services. The lawful basis for processing is the performance of a contract and, where applicable, legitimate interests in providing effective coaching support.

In the course of coaching, some information shared may be considered sensitive personal data. Such information is processed only with your explicit consent.

Your data is stored securely — electronically and/or in paper format — and is not shared with third parties except where required by law or where necessary for the provision of services, such as secure data storage or communication platforms.

Section 04

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • →Contract — processing necessary to deliver the coaching services you have engaged
  • →Legitimate interests — maintaining professional records and communicating about our coaching relationship
  • →Legal obligation — retaining records where required by law
  • →Consent — where you have explicitly agreed to specific uses of your data
Section 05

Third party tools and data processors

We use the following third party tools to deliver our services. Each acts as a data processor on our behalf and is GDPR compliant:

  • →Calendly — used for booking and scheduling discovery calls and coaching sessions. Calendly collects your name and email address to schedule meetings. Calendly is GDPR compliant and includes a UK Data Processing Addendum. See calendly.com/legal/privacy-notice for details.
  • →123-reg / Titan Mail — used to operate our professional email address info@samuelanguscoach.co.uk. Email communications are stored on their secure servers.
  • →GitHub Pages — used to host this website. Standard server logs may be collected. See GitHub's privacy policy for details.
  • →Google Meet — used to conduct online coaching sessions. Google processes data in accordance with its privacy policy and GDPR commitments.

We do not transfer your personal data to any other third parties without your explicit consent.

Section 06

How we store and protect your data

Your personal data is stored securely using the following measures:

  • →Session notes and client records are stored on a password-protected encrypted device
  • →Email communications are stored on a secure professional email platform
  • →Booking data is held within Calendly's secure, GDPR-compliant platform
  • →Access to all client data is restricted to Samuel Angus only

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and inform you directly where required by law.

Section 07

How long we keep your data

We retain your personal data for a period of three years following the end of our coaching relationship. After this period your data will be securely deleted.

Financial records, including payment information, are retained for seven years in accordance with HMRC requirements.

You may request early deletion of your data at any time — see Your Rights below.

Section 08

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • →Right of access — to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • →Right to rectification — to request correction of inaccurate data
  • →Right to erasure — to request deletion of your data where there is no legitimate reason to retain it
  • →Right to restrict processing — to request that we limit how we use your data
  • →Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • →Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • →Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, to withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights please contact us using the details below. We will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with our response you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

Section 09

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies necessary for its operation. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. Calendly, when accessed through our booking link, may use its own cookies in accordance with its privacy policy.

Section 10

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practice or legal requirements. The date at the top of this page will always show when it was last updated. Significant changes will be communicated to active clients directly.
