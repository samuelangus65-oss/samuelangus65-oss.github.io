Partner
They become an advocate
Transformation invites others — a client who has moved forward naturally brings the next person.
▪Referral system — every satisfied client asked for three introductions
▪Referral reward — a free session for any introduction that becomes a client

The principles holding it together

▪Relationships before reach — warm introductions convert faster than any advertising.
▪Lead with the person — people search for someone in their corner who understands what they are navigating, not for a coach.
▪Value before the ask — give generously; the invitation comes only after trust.
▪Consistency over intensity — a few stages worked steadily beat many done in bursts.
▪Honour rest — the Sabbath is protected; the system pauses so you do not have to.
